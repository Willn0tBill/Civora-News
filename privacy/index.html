Privacy

Reader preferences, advertising services, and your privacy choices.

Updated November 9, 2026. This page explains data used by Civora's public reading experience and third-party services. It does not imply that Google has approved this site for advertising.

Reader preferences stored on this device

Civora does not currently offer public reader accounts or an active newsletter subscriber database. On-device storage may hold your light/dark theme, saved article identifiers, and recently opened article identifiers. Archive search runs in the browser, although your search words may appear in the URL when you submit it. Civora does not upload the locally saved reading list or history as a reader profile.

Clearing this information affects this device only; it does not delete third-party advertising cookies or hosting logs.

Advertising and cookies

Civora embeds Google AdSense code under publisher ID ca-pub-1423460909106679. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies and similar identifiers to serve advertisements based on earlier visits to Civora and other websites. Google and its advertising partners may use visit, device and browser information for ad delivery, measurement and, where permitted, personalization. Google may receive technical request data such as an IP address when its scripts load, even if no visible ad is filled. Actual advertising depends on approval, eligibility, browser settings, and regional consent requirements.

You can control or opt out of personalized Google ads using Google My Ad Center. For some other participating ad vendors, visit YourAdChoices. These controls may not eliminate all ads, and browser privacy controls or extensions may also limit cookies. Read Google's advertising privacy information for details.

Consent and regional choices

Where law requires consent, including relevant European advertising requirements, an appropriate certified consent-management platform and regional choices must be configured through the advertising provider. A privacy-policy link alone is not consent. The presence of the AdSense script does not prove that a consent message is active or that the site has been approved. Readers should be able to use the editorial pages whether or not an ad is filled.

Hosting and content delivery

GitHub Pages hosts Civora's static pages and may process technical request information through its hosting systems. Google Fonts is requested for typography and may receive device/network request information. Parts of the live news feed and dynamic story reader request public content from Supabase; those network services may process technical connection information. Those requests are distinct from Civora's on-device saved-story and reading-history features. External source links lead to sites with their own privacy practices.

Newsroom and policy changes

Staff publishing tools use separate restricted access and are not public reader accounts. We will revise this disclosure if the site's collection, subscriptions, analytics, advertising vendors or consent setup changes. For publication and correction availability, see the Contact and Corrections pages; no private tip-submission channel is currently offered.
