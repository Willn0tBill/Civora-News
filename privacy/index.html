Privacy

A minimal first version without third-party advertising trackers or newsletter signup forms.

What this site collects

The public newspaper does not use reader accounts or a newsletter subscription database. Search filters the article archive in your browser; submitting a search may place the query in the page URL. Theme preferences can be stored in your browser’s local storage.

Hosting and outside links

GitHub Pages and internet intermediaries may handle routine technical request data. External links are operated by their respective websites and follow their own privacy practices.

Future changes

Authorized newsroom editors use a separate Studio interface. Until dedicated staff sign-in is enabled, that tool uses GitHub credentials supplied by the editor, and unsent drafts stay in that browser’s local storage. This is not a public reader registration system. Any future analytics, advertising or subscriptions require an updated disclosure.
