TRANSPARENCY

Privacy

Reader preferences, advertising services, and your privacy choices.

What this site collects

The public newspaper does not use reader accounts or a newsletter subscription database. Search filters the article archive in your browser; submitting a search may place the query in the page URL. Theme preferences, optional saved stories, and recently opened article identifiers may be stored in your browser’s local storage. You can clear them through browser site-data controls. These reading features do not upload your reading history to Civora.

Advertising and cookies

Civora has added the Google AdSense advertising script (publisher ID ca-pub-1423460909106679). If advertising is enabled, Google and advertising partners may use cookies or similar technology to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize advertisements. Whether ads display depends on approval, regional requirements, consent settings, and browser preferences. Advertising requests may transmit technical information, including an IP address and browser details, to Google. Learn more in Google’s advertising privacy information.

Where advertising consent is required, Civora must configure an appropriate consent-management platform and obtain the necessary choices before serving relevant ads. The AdSense tag itself does not mean the site has been approved or that advertisements are currently displaying.

Hosting and outside links

GitHub Pages and internet intermediaries may handle routine technical request data. External links are operated by their respective websites and follow their own privacy practices.

Newsroom access and updates

Approved staff use a separate login-enabled newsroom to manage articles. Civora does not offer public reader registration. This disclosure will be updated if advertising, analytics or subscription practices materially change.
