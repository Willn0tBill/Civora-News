HOW WE WORK

Editorial Standards

The audience deserves verifiable information, clear attribution and visible corrections.

Evidence comes before a headline

We check dates, units, definitions, denominators, publication context and named sources before presenting a finding as fact. Whenever possible, we link primary documents so a reader can trace a claim back to its origin. An official statistic can be reliable while its interpretation still requires scrutiny.

What is original at Civora?

Civora's initial edition consists of independently written, source-based summaries, explainers, comparisons and analytical interpretations, not eyewitness or interview-based reporting. Original analysis means showing the reasoning between evidence and a conclusion, distinguishing the source's finding from Civora's inference, and making limitations explicit. Restating someone else's press release does not by itself establish new reporting.

Statistics and uncertainty

For a statistic, we aim to say who counted, what was counted, over which period, and what was excluded or revised. Reported fraud losses are not every loss suffered; a yearly climate figure is not a long-run trend; and internet coverage is not the same as internet use. Derived comparisons should identify their inputs and rounding.

Independent judgment and conflicts

Advertisers do not direct reporting, and sponsored material must be conspicuously labeled. We do not claim anonymous interviews, access to confidential documents, fieldwork or new measurements that did not occur. Sources and editorial methods should be distinguishable from opinions and assumptions.

Accuracy and corrections

We correct verified material errors, explain significant changes and preserve a visible dated corrections record. An official newsroom contact method is still pending, and we do not ask readers to send private information through public development channels.

AI assistance

The first edition was drafted with AI assistance and checked against the primary documents linked in its articles. Generated prose is not evidence. We do not publish invented quotations, sources or events. Where a source does not support a new detail, we omit it or state the limitation.

Copyright and imagery

Images and other media must be original, public domain or properly licensed. Linking or crediting a copyrighted work does not automatically grant reproduction rights.
