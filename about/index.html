About Civora

Independent by design. Interested in substance over noise.

What Civora is

Civora News is an independent digital publication under development. We aim to cover U.S. affairs, world events, technology, business, science and culture with thoughtful context and transparent sourcing.

Our present launch library contains clearly marked demonstration explainers. It is not a staffed breaking-news operation and does not claim original interviews or live reporting.

Our commitments

Accuracy before speed. Clear separation of news, opinion and advertising. Honest corrections. Respect for source material and original reporting. No invented quotes or staged reporting presented as fact.

Who runs it?

The publication is currently an independent project. A public masthead and newsroom contact will be added as appropriate before commissioning outside reporting.
