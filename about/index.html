About Civora

Independent by design. Interested in substance over noise.

What Civora is

Civora News is an independent digital publication covering U.S. affairs, world developments, technology, business, science and culture through source-based reports, original explainers and careful attribution.

The initial published edition draws from publicly available materials of agencies and scientific organizations. These articles were researched and drafted with AI assistance; their primary sources are linked for readers to inspect. We do not claim original interviews, field reporting or scientific measurements that were not conducted.

Our commitments

Accuracy before speed. Clear separation of news, opinion and advertising. Honest corrections. Respect for source material and original reporting. No invented quotes or staged reporting presented as fact.

Who runs it?

The publication is currently an independent project. A public masthead and newsroom contact will be added as appropriate before commissioning outside reporting.
